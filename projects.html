MoonStore

A multi-tenant SaaS platform giving African Instagram, Threads and WhatsApp vendors their own personalised online store in minutes. Built solo with the MERN stack, real-time chat (Socket.IO), and Paystack-powered payments with automated subaccount splits. Sellers manage products through a dashboard while buyers browse products, order, chat and pay directly on the platform, no WhatsApp required. Founded and built end-to-end, from architecture to first paying customers.

Fade King

Fade King is a fast, single-page website built for a barber shop to solve a common problem: potential clients scrolling past a business with no real online presence. Instead of a slow, generic template, Fade King gives the shop a sharp, static site where visitors can see the barber's work in the gallery, know pricing upfront, read the shop's story, and find contact details all without a single loading delay. Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and deployed on Vercel.
